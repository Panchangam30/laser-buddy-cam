## Part images

`web/public/parts/*.png` are rendered by `npm run thumbs` from the 3D parts in
`src/three/kitModels.tsx`. An image of a part drawn with one of the models above
(the Uno, the servo, LEDs, resistors, buttons, the potentiometer, photoresistor,
LCD, seven-segment display, buzzer, ICs, capacitors, TO-92 parts and the relay)
is a render of that model and carries the same licence and attribution. Images
of parts drawn by this project's own code (wires, motors, battery, remote,
joystick and the other primitives) are covered by the project's MIT License.
